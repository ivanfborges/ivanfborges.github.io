Geospatial machine learning · Rio de Janeiro

Can listing characteristics and location estimate advertised Airbnb prices?

The study keeps hosts separate between training and evaluation, compares baselines with four ML candidates, and examines geographic behavior. The final model and its inference interface are frozen.

BRL 482.67final MAE vs. BRL 620.57 room-type baseline · 22.22% lower

The result describes one Rio snapshot and advertised nightly prices. It does not establish transaction value, future pricing or production suitability.

Classification · Kaggle study

How well does a reproducible classifier predict the historical case label?

TopVistos compares seven model configurations. Preprocessing stays inside training folds, and the 0.60 approval threshold was selected on validation data before the final holdout was evaluated.

0.701macro F1 on 3,568 final-holdout cases · 95% bootstrap interval 0.686–0.717

Errors vary across education groups. This historical-label study is not validated for visa eligibility or automated immigration decisions.

03 / How the work is evaluated

Decisions that can be inspected.

The case studies document their evaluation choices and the boundary between recorded results and future possibilities.

01

Separate development from final evidence

Thresholds and model choices are made before the final holdout. Airbnb also separates hosts to reduce overlap between training and evaluation.

02

Make the workflow traceable

Protocols, reports, automated tests and inference examples let readers inspect how each result was produced and used.

03

State where evidence stops

Snapshot scope, segment errors, synthetic examples and missing production validation are explicit parts of the work.
